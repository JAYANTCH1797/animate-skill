# Style: bee-dance

**In one sentence:** a pink watercolour comb on cream paper where one white line, danced by a painted bee, opens up into a sunny meadow, because the whole point of the waggle dance is that a line drawn indoors is a real route outdoors.

## What each reference gives us
- **Koi pond reel, first 12 s** (library card reused): the opening move (a pink drop on bare paper blooms until it nearly fills the frame, paper margin kept); the mottled hot-pink wash; white line work and ripple rings; tiny coloured confetti; one short mono caps caption at a time; a painted creature with a soft shadow as the star.
- **Blossom still**: colour only. The sky blue, cream light, blossom pink, yellow-leaning greens and the orange accent. They run the payoff meadow and the sun and honey on the comb.

## What we will not copy
Koi, lily pads, lotus, their fish layout, the handle or the Instagram mark. From the blossom still: no train, bench, wires or anime cel shading. The meadow is painted as watercolour so both halves share one hand.

## Type
IBM Plex Mono SemiBold from `fonts/`, caps, slightly tracked, dark ink, centred at about 78% height. Same job as the reel's captions, not the same font. Diagram labels ("30°", "1 KM") in the same face.

## Texture
Paper: mottled tan with faint horizontal fibre and browned corners, fixed. Washes: low blooms, fine granulation, pigment pooled in a dark band at every edge. Comb cells are "wax resist": cell interiors slightly darker, walls left pale, so the comb sits in the wash rather than on it. A fixed grain is multiplied over everything so the bee and line sit in the paper.

## Motion feel
Calm, fast, calm. Bloom eases out. The bee's waggle is the one busy thing; everything else drifts. Comb stays still; the wash never crawls. The change of pace is the drama: a full stop before the payoff, then the meadow opens along the line.

## One device
**One white line that never breaks.** It starts as the ripple where the drop lands, is flown in by the bee, is danced as a figure-eight, and at the payoff simply keeps going at the same angle, out across the meadow to the blossom. The geometry is literal: the sun, the 30° wedge and the line never move when the world changes around them.
