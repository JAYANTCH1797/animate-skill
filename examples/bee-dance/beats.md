## Fact check
- Foragers dance on the vertical comb in the dark hive; straight up stands for the direction of the sun, and the angle off vertical is the angle off the sun. Solid (von Frisch; NC State Extension).
- Figure-eight with alternating return loops. Solid.
- Waggle-run duration encodes distance. Solid. **"About 1 second per km" is an average**: each bee has her own calibration and it varies by landscape. Caption says "ABOUT". Flag in hand-over.
- Followers get a rough fix, not a pin: scatter grows with distance and they finish the job by scent. "HER SISTERS FLY IT" is simplified; the film shows them going straight there.
- Real waggle is about 13-15 shakes a second. Drawn at 6 a second so it reads at 30 fps.
